# Services and CTA Specification

## Overview
- Target: `src/components/ServicesSection.tsx`
- Screenshot: `docs/design-references/giacong-home-desktop-loaded.png`
- Interaction: static cards with hover elevation.

## Structure
- White centered section around 950px desktop height with heading followed by four equal cards.
- Cards are pale green (#eff8e8), 16px radius, service icon, title, description, check-list and outlined `Xem thêm` button.
- CTA below spans 400px desktop / 355px mobile. Green patterned background, left copy/buttons and food image on the right.

## Content
- Services: Nghiên cứu, Sản xuất, Thiết kế, Đóng gói.
- CTA: Sẵn sàng tiếp cận hàng triệu khách hàng tiềm năng?
- Buttons: Chat với chuyên gia; Yêu cầu tư vấn.

## Responsive
- Four service cards become 2 columns at 390px; CTA keeps an image crop right while text occupies the left half.
